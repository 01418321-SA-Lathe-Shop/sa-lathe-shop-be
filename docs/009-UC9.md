## 1. Fulfillment Lifecycle & Owner-Confirmed Policies

UC9 implements the final phase of customer fulfillment: setting invoice credit terms, issuing post-deposit invoices, recording delivery dispatch, customer inspection (acceptance or return for free rework), installment payments, and final request closure. All seven policies were confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-20.

### 1. Invoicing Rules
- **Admin Credit Terms**: Admin explicitly sets `credit_days` (0–36,500) once on a completed job. There is no default.
- **One Invoice Per Job**: The invoice amount is fixed at `job.balance` ($\text{Quoted Price} - \text{Actual Approved Deposit}$).
- **Due Date**: $\text{Due Instant} = \text{Invoice Issued At} + (\text{credit\_days} \times 24 \text{ hours})$.
- **Worker View**: Workers see the invoice balance required for delivery verification, but never internal production costs or payment receipt rows.

### 2. Whole-Job Delivery & Customer Inspection
- **Dispatch**: Admin or Worker records shipment (`method = self` or `third_party`) with a required `receiver_name`. Partial shipments are not supported.
- **Customer Inspection**: The customer inspects the delivered parts and submits an inspection decision:
  - **Accept**: Sets delivery status to `delivered`. Acceptance is final.
  - **Return (Free Pre-Acceptance Rework)**: Requires a mandatory reason. Atomically archives the completed cycle, increments `production_cycle`, and reopens the same job in `in_progress` status.

### 3. Free Rework Rules
- Customer return reopens the job for free rework without changing the original invoice amount, payment records, or customer price.
- **No Stock Re-crediting**: Previously issued materials are not returned to usable stock. Additional materials consumed during rework must be issued from free stock under UC8.
- Production must be marked `completed` again before staff can record a new redelivery attempt.

### 4. Customer Payments & Installments
- Customers can pay in multiple installments. Each bank payment submission requires an amount $\le$ current unpaid balance, payment timestamp, and ready private slip evidence.
- **Admin Payment Review**: Customer bank submissions do not reduce the balance until Admin explicitly reviews and approves them. Rejection requires an audited reason.
- **Cash Receipts**: Admin can directly record cash receipts with optional proof.
- Overpayment and unallocated customer credit are strictly prohibited.

### 5. Request Closure
- The parent `job_request` is marked `completed` **only when BOTH conditions are met**:
  1. The latest delivery is accepted by the customer (`delivered`).
  2. The invoice is fully paid (balance = 0.00).
- Delivery before final payment is permitted, and payment before delivery is permitted.

## 2. API Endpoints

All commands require an `idempotency_key` and the current `expected_version` from the fulfillment response.

| Method | Path | Permission | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/fulfillment/jobs` | Staff / Customer | List fulfillment jobs with pagination |
| `GET` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}` | Staff / Customer | Detailed fulfillment state (payments redacted for Workers) |
| `PUT` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/credit-terms` | Admin | Set invoice credit days |
| `POST` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/invoice` | Admin / Worker | Issue post-deposit customer invoice |
| `POST` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/deliveries` | Admin / Worker | Record delivery shipment |
| `POST` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/inspection` | Customer Member | Record customer inspection (accept or return) |
| `POST` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/payments/bank` | Customer Member | Submit bank installment with private payment slip |
| `POST` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/payments/cash` | Admin | Record cash payment receipt |
| `POST` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/payments/review` | Admin | Approve or reject pending bank payment |
| `POST` | `/fulfillment/jobs/{job_id}/evidence/uploads` | Customer / Admin | Request upload intent for payment proof |
| `POST` | `/customer-evidence/{evidence_id}/complete` | Uploader | Complete and freeze payment proof in S3 |
| `GET` | `/customer-evidence/{evidence_id}/download` | Uploader / Admin | Download signed private payment proof |

## 3. Database Schema & Migration 000012

- `customer_invoice`: Stores invoice number, total amount, due date, status (`unpaid`, `partial`, `paid`), and credit days.
- `job_delivery`: Records whole-job delivery attempts, shipping method, and receiver name.
- `job_delivery_inspection`: Stores customer acceptance or return reasons.
- `payment`: Records bank and cash payments with review status (`pending`, `approved`, `rejected`).
- `customer_payment_allocation`: Records exact approved allocations against the invoice.
- `customer_evidence`: Stores S3 keys and metadata for private payment slips.
- `rework_history`: Audited cycles linking reopened jobs to previous completions and returns.

## 6. Use-Case Description Alignment (2026-11-03)

Source: `Use_Case_Description_UC1-UC12.md`. Conflicts are listed in `025-uc-spec-alignment.md`.

Matches the description: the invoice covers the remaining balance (final price minus the deposit); one invoice per job, not reissued after a return; delivery moves from shipping to delivered or returned; a returned job goes back to production; several installments are allocated to the invoice; overpayment is refused; the request is closed when accepted and paid (Q9.6).

Conflicts kept at the confirmed rule:
- Delivery method: the description allows `pickup` and records a `shipping_cost`. The implementation supports `self` and `third_party` with a required receiver name and no shipping cost.
- Payment recording: the description has the Technician record the payment (Q9.4). The customer submits bank evidence, Admin reviews it, and Admin records cash.
- Due date: the description takes a due date as input. The confirmed rule is Admin-set credit days, with due time = issued time + credit days × 24 hours.
- Invoice status: the description stores a status column. The implementation derives `unpaid`, `partial` or `paid` from payment allocations, as the confirmed rule requires.
