# UC4 — Deposit/PO Evidence Review & Job Opening

## 1. Business Workflow & Operational Policies

UC4 governs the customer submission and staff verification of deposit payments and purchase orders (PO), followed by the atomic opening of production jobs.

### Workflow Summary
1. **Fulfillment Inquiry**: Customer retrieves terms via `GET /quotations/{id}/fulfillment`.
2. **Private File Upload**: Customer uploads evidence file via `POST /requests/{id}/uploads`, signed PUT to S3, and `POST /uploads/{id}/complete` (under UC1 private upload rules).
3. **Evidence Submission**: Customer binds the completed private attachment to the quotation as `deposit` or `po` evidence.
4. **Staff Manual Review**: Either an **Admin** or a **Worker** reviews the private evidence download and approves or rejects it.
5. **Atomic Job Opening**: Upon final required approval (both PO and deposit if required, or single requirement), the system atomically opens **exactly one queued job**.

### Owner-Confirmed Policies
- **Manual Verification**: Uploading a slip is not money verification. Staff manually check banking slips or corporate PO documents.
- **Deposit Flexibility**: A single deposit payment may range from the 50% rounded minimum up to 100% of the total price. Multiple small installment deposits are not accepted in UC4.
- **Actual Deposit Deduction**: The job's opening balance equals `final_price - actual_approved_deposit`. A 100% deposit leaves a 0.00 balance.
- **Single Job Cardinality**: Exactly one queued job opens per approved quotation/request (overriding the older ER's 1-to-many model).
- **14-Day Deadline Protection**: Complete timely evidence submitted before `documents_due_at` (accepted_at + 336 hours) is protected; staff may review it after the deadline without triggering cancellation.
- **Automatic Expiry**: If required evidence is missing or remains rejected after the deadline, the background scanner cancels the request. Rejected evidence can be replaced with a new submission before the deadline.

## 2. API Endpoints

| Method | Path | Permission | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/quotations/{quotation_id}/evidence` | Customer Member | Submit completed attachment as deposit or PO evidence |
| `POST` | `/evidence/{evidence_id}/review` | Admin or Worker | Approve or reject submitted evidence |
| `GET` | `/quotations/{quotation_id}/fulfillment` | Customer / Staff | Get acceptance terms, evidence list, and job status |
| `GET` | `/attachments/{attachment_id}/download` | Authorized Reader | Generate short-lived signed link to inspect evidence |

### Example Payloads

**1. Submit Deposit Evidence (`POST /quotations/{id}/evidence`)**:
```json
{
  "idempotency_key": "c56a4180-65aa-42ec-a945-5fd21dec0538",
  "attachment_id": "9b1deb4d-3b7d-4bad-9bdd-2b0d7b3dcb6d",
  "kind": "deposit",
  "amount": "1000.00",
  "paid_at": "2026-10-18T03:00:00Z"
}
```

**2. Submit PO Evidence (`POST /quotations/{id}/evidence`)**:
```json
{
  "idempotency_key": "d8203f71-29bc-43be-9233-149f7e504094",
  "attachment_id": "a42918b9-43c2-48a1-b844-315ec0f61f71",
  "kind": "po"
}
```

**3. Staff Review (`POST /evidence/{id}/review`)**:
```json
{
  "outcome": "approved",
  "expected_version": 1
}
```
*For rejection, set `"outcome": "rejected"` and supply `"reason": "PO document missing corporate stamp"`.*

## 3. Deadline Scanning & Cancellation

- The background worker (`cmd/worker`) scans for expired submissions every 15 seconds.
- Requests with overdue missing evidence are locked and transitioned to `canceled` with an entry in `uc4_cancellation`.
- A review-versus-expiry race condition serializes on the request row lock, ensuring timely submitted evidence is never prematurely canceled.

---

## 4. Database Schema & Migration 000007

- `deposit` and `purchase_order` (ER DEPOSIT, PURCHASE_ORDER; migration 000023, replacing `term_evidence`, which remains as a read-only union view): store `quotation_id`, `kind` (`deposit`/`po`), `amount`+`paid_at` (deposit only), `status` (`pending`/`approved`/`rejected`), reviewer, and review timestamps.
- `job`: Created with `status = 'queued'`, `total_price`, `deposit_paid`, and `balance`.
- `uc4_cancellation`: Records system cancellation audits when deadlines expire.

## 6. Use-Case Description Alignment (2026-11-03)

Source: `Use_Case_Description_UC1-UC12.md`. Conflicts are listed in `025-uc-spec-alignment.md`.

Matches the description: deposit for individuals and for companies above THB 10,000; PO only for companies at or below THB 10,000; the job is created `queued` only after all required approvals; missing evidence is found by a separate scheduled check.

Described but not implemented as written:
- Automatic check of the submitted amount against the due amount (step 3.4) with no staff review. Every submission is reviewed by Admin or Worker.
- Estimated cost and estimated time on the job (Q4.3). Not in the schema.
- A "hold" state for requests past their deadline (Q4.E1). Missing evidence expires after 336 hours instead.

Conflicts kept at the confirmed rule:
- Deposit amount: the description uses `ROUND(50%, 2)`. The confirmed rule rounds a 50% deposit up to a satang.
- Deposit amount: the description requires an exact match with the due amount. The confirmed rule accepts a single deposit from the rounded 50% minimum through 100%.
- Deadline: the description leaves the period open. The confirmed period is 14 days (336 hours) after quotation acceptance.
